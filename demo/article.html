The art of noticing

A five-minute pause, by the Fieldnotes team

The world is full of small wonders. We just need to slow down enough to see them. A patch of sunlight on the kitchen floor can be the beginning of an adventure.

You do not need to travel far to find something extraordinary. Sometimes, a familiar path offers a completely new perspective. Curiosity turns an ordinary walk into a journey.

Attention is a form of generosity. What we notice becomes part of who we are.

Start with one small thing

Dr. Morgan keeps a notebook by the window. At 8.30 a.m., she writes down three things she has never noticed before. The experiment costs $0.50 and takes just five minutes.

Ask a question. Listen carefully! What surprised you today? You may find that the answers are closer than you think.
